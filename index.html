Favicon Generator

Design an icon from text, an emoji or an image, then download everything a website needs. It all happens in your browser, so your image never leaves your device.

Source
Text

Up to 3 characters.

Emoji look different on each system. "Same everywhere" downloads an emoji font (1.9 MB) the first time.

Icon

Icons from Lucide (ISC license), bundled with this page. The icon color is the same setting as the text color.

Image

PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP. A square image works best.

Shape and background
Border

A share of the icon size, so it looks the same at every size. Small icons keep a thin border of at least 1 px.

Position

Some letters and logos look off-center even when they are centered. Nudge them here.

Dark mode

Only favicon.svg can change in dark mode, so the dark browser tab in the preview shows these colors. The PNG and ICO files keep the normal colors.

Android and site

Space around your design in the Android maskable icon. Add more if the shapes in the preview cut it off.

Preview

    Android shapes

    Android cuts the maskable icon to the shape the phone uses. Everything inside the dashed safe zone always shows. Choose a shape to use it on the Android home screen preview.
